## Key decisions
- Undirected graph; edge weight must be >= 3D straight-line distance (A* admissible); loader warns otherwise.
- Floor height 4 m; floor transitions are ordinary weighted edges.
- Nodes explored = nodes settled (popped, non-stale). A101->B202: Dijkstra 12, A* 10, distance 36 m.
- Timing: avg of 2000 runs; A* not faster in wall time on this tiny map (honest, documented).
